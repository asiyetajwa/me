// Questions[  ]

    

      

        
Good to know.

        
Can't find your answer? Our reception team is happy to help.

        Call +254 722 784 882
      

      

        

          How do I book an appointment?
          
Call reception on +254 722 784 882, or send a request with your preferred day and time. Our reception team will get back to you to find a date and time that works best for you.

        

        

          I'm in pain. How quickly can I be seen?
          
Please call us rather than sending a request, so we can find you the earliest available appointment. We are open from 07:00, Monday to Saturday.

        

        

          Do you see children?
          
Yes. We offer specialised care for children and make dental visits fun and educational, so little ones grow up comfortable at the dentist. Many of our families bring three generations.

        

        

          I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?
          
Absolutely. Let us know when you book and we will take things at your pace, explain every step and make sure you feel comfortable before we begin.

        

        

          Do you accept insurance?
          
Please call reception with your insurer's details and we will confirm your cover before your visit, so there are no surprises.

        

        

          Where are you, and when are you open?
          
We are on James Gichuru Road in Lavington, Nairobi, open Monday to Saturday from 07:00 to 17:00. Get directions.
